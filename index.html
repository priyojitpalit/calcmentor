SPRING HILL COLLEGE · MTH 121

Two calculus experiments

Change the mathematical parameters and observe the consequences. The plots are computed in your browser.

1. Secant lines and the derivative

For f(x) = x², the secant line joins (a, f(a)) to (a+h, f(a+h)). The green line is the tangent. Investigate what happens as h approaches zero.

Your browser does not support interactive canvas.

Blue = f(x), orange = secant line, green = tangent line. For this example the secant slope is 2a + h while f′(a) = 2a.

2. Riemann sums and definite integrals

Approximate ∫ from 0 to 2 of (x² + 1) dx using right-endpoint rectangles. Increase the number of rectangles to see convergence toward the exact integral.
