    How CalcMentor works

    CalcMentor is an entirely free, browser-based calculus coach. It uses programmed mathematical rules and numerical checks—not a paid chatbot or remote AI service.

    The checker understands common Calc I expressions, including powers, polynomials, products, quotients, trigonometry, logarithms and basic compositions. It tests equivalent expressions at multiple points. This is not a formal proof system and may not recognize every valid answer.

    Your practice totals stay in this browser's local storage. They are not submitted to an instructor or sent to a server. Use the hints and worked solutions as learning aids, and verify important results independently.
